Jollof Rice

Description

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish know for its rich colour, deicious flavour, distinctive aroma, its easy method of preparation amongst others. Below is a list of the ingredients needed to prepare jollof rice.

  • Rice
  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Onions
  • Red bell peppers
  • Scotch bonnet peppers
  • Cooking oil
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Salt
  • Curry powder
  • Thyme
  • Chicken stock
  • Seasoning cubes (optional)

Steps Involved In Making The Dish.

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, onions, garlic, and ginger.
  3. Heat the cooking oil in a pan and fry some chopped onions.
  4. Add the tomatoe paste to the pan and stir fry for a few minutes till evenly mixed.
  5. Pour in the blended mixture and cook until the sauce thickens and excess water reduces.
  6. Add salt, curry powder, thyme, and seasoning cubes to taste.
  7. Add the chicken stock and bring the sauce to boil.
  8. Add the washed rice and stir until it is evenly coated with the sauce.
  9. Cover the pot and cook over low heat until rice is tender, adding little water if necessary.
  10. Fluff the rice gently with a fork and serve hot
