Bread and Omelette

Description

This is a popular meal made with bread and fried eggs.


Basic Ingredients

  • Eggs: 2 to 3 large eggs.
  • Bread: 2 slices of white or whole-wheat bread.
  • Veggies: Finely chopped onions, tomatoes, green chilies, and fresh coriander (cilantro).
  • Spices & Seasoning: Salt, black pepper, cumin powder, or chaat masala.
  • Fat: Butter or oil for the pan.
  • Optional: Cheese slices or green chutney.

How to Make It

  1. Whisk the eggs: Beat the eggs in a bowl with salt, pepper, and your choice of spices. Mix in the chopped onions, tomatoes, and chilies.
  2. Cook the base: Heat butter or oil in a pan over medium-low heat. Pour the egg mixture in and spread it out.
  3. Add the bread: Place two slices of bread right onto the wet egg layer, then flip them over so both sides get coated.
  4. Fold and toast: Once the egg sets and browns, fold the edges of the omelette over the bread, add cheese if desired, and fold the slices into a sandwich. Toast both sides until golden.
