Fried Plantain


Description

Fried plantain is a popular dish made from ripe plantain fried until golden brown.

ingredients

  • Ripe plantain
  • Cooking oil
  • salt (optional)

Steps

  1. Peel the plantain and cut into slices.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a pan.
  3. Fry the slices until golden brown.
  4. Remove the olantain and drain excess oil.
